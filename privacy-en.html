1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application handles your privacy. We are committed to ensuring maximum protection for your personal data, particularly information regarding your menstrual cycle and health.

2. Data Collection and Storage

The application is designed around the local storage principle. All data you enter into the application (cycle dates, notes, tags, settings) is stored exclusively on your device using SwiftData technology. We do not collect, store, or transfer your medical or personal data to third-party server databases.

3. Use of Information

Since your data remains on your device, it is used solely to provide the core functionality of the application: cycle prediction, statistics display, and saving your personal notes.

4. Third Parties

We do not transfer, sell, or exchange your personal data with third parties. The application does not use third-party marketing trackers or analytics systems that collect identifiable health information.

5. Data Security

The security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using lock screen protection (Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode) to prevent unauthorized access.
